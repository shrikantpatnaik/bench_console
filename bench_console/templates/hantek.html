Capture and measurement details

Triggers are software edge triggers within finite USB captures; there are gaps between records. Auto displays a record even without a matching edge. Normal waits for a matching edge; Single stops only after a matching edge. The last triggered record remains visible while waiting.

AC and GND coupling are software display operations. AC subtracts each record’s mean. Probe attenuation must match the physical probe. Position and invert affect the display; position does not change measured values.

Hardware scope acquisition discards 4,096 ADC startup samples per channel before triggering or measuring. The capture label shows ADC startup discarded when the helper verifies that count.

Voltage measurements use the visible record. Timing requires at least two complete, sufficiently sampled periods; when the view is too short, timing uses the longer captured record and the plot identifies the affected channels. A dash means insufficient or invalid data. Top/base and overshoot/preshoot are waveform-dependent estimates. Timing at a small number of samples per cycle is approximate.

Standard sigrok support provides two analog channels or eight logic channels, separately. H/P pressed selects scope; released selects logic. Disconnect before switching, and replug/rescan if the device does not change personality. No 16-channel mode or hardware trigger is claimed.
